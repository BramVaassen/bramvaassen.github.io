I am an associate professor in philosophy at Umeå University, Sweden.

Most of my research centres around causation: what it is and how what it is shapes or constrains arguments in philosophy. I have published work on why causal arguments for physicalism are not as good as they seem, on why a naive semantics of ‘therefore’ is better than it seems, and on why treating chatbots as humans is exactly as undignified as it seems.

Before taking up my current position, I was a postdoctoral research fellow at Rutgers University, Umeå University, and the University of Cologne, and obtained my PhD at Umeå University.
