Bram Vaassen

I am an associate professor at Umeå University, where I research philosophical issues surrounding causation and explanation, and teach across metaphysics, philosophy of mind, metaethics, and the pedagogical use of philosophical puzzles.

Before taking up this position, I was a postdoctoral research fellow at Rutgers University, Umeå University, and the University of Cologne, and obtained my PhD at Umeå University.

When I'm not working, I am trying to develop a sense of style.
