I am an associate professor in philosophy at Umeå University. My research centres around causation and explanation. I teach courses in, among other things, metaphysics, philosophy of mind, and on teaching philosophy itself.

Before taking up this position, I was a postdoctoral research fellow at Rutgers University, Umeå University, and the University of Cologne, and obtained my PhD at Umeå University.
