I am an associate professor of philosophy at Umeå University.

I work on the intersection of metaphysics and philosophy of science, with a particular interest in causation and explanation. My research investigates whether causal facts, such as the fact that my being in pain causes me to complain endlessly, can tell us anything about the metaphysical status of the phenomena involved, such as my pain being physical or rather made up of 'mental' stuff. Overall, I am pessimistic about causation as a guide to metaphysics.

Before taking up this position, I was a postdoctoral research fellow at Rutgers University, Umeå University, and the University of Cologne, and obtained my PhD at Umeå University.
