I am an associate professor in philosophy at Umeå University, Sweden

I work mainly on the metaphysics of causation, with off-shoots in philosophy of mind, philosophy of language, and philosophy of AI.

Before taking up this position, I was a postdoctoral research fellow at Rutgers University, Umeå University, and the University of Cologne, and obtained my PhD at Umeå University.
